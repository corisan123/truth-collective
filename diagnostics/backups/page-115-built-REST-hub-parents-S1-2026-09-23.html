How We Evaluate Smart Lighting

Light Is Not an Aesthetic Decision. It Is an Engineering One.

Every product in the Smart Lighting hub is evaluated against IES photometric standards and CIE circadian lighting criteria. A minimum Color Rendering Index of 90+ is required for recommendation, without exception. Products are assessed for color temperature accuracy, dimming compatibility, smart protocol support, and electrical safety compliance under UL, ETL, FCC, and DOE ENERGY STAR frameworks.

Stage I disqualifies any product that cannot document photometric accuracy or that fails basic electrical safety certification. Stage II scores performance numerically across circadian alignment, driver quality, color accuracy, and integration capability. The scoring model weights component engineering at 30%. In lighting, that means driver materials, dimmer circuit quality, and semiconductor grade.

What is marketed as smart lighting and what meets IES and CIE standards are frequently different products. This hub only contains the ones that passed both.
