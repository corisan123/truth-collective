Multi-Stage Selection Standards

Every product featured across Truth Collective is reviewed against Truth Collective's trusted selection processes before it earns a place on the page. All featured items must meet a minimum (3.5/5) star rating with a record of long-term reviews, demonstrate build quality and brand track record worthy of daily professional use, do one job better than alternatives at their price point, retain long-term value, and remain available through a trusted retailer with a sensible return path and elevated customer service.

Truth Collective grades reflect our independent review processes. While some products may display different ratings on retailer pages, Truth Collective grades are assigned through our own independent testing, research, and editorial review, evaluated against our Multi-Stage Selections Standards Processes. We firmly stand behind our rankings as honest, sophisticated using proprietary software making our independent assessments created and featured for the long-term trust of our readers.

Truth Collective also follows the Federal Trade Commission's endorsement and testimonial guidelines. Every recommendation is honest, every affiliate relationship is disclosed, and no product receives a placement it did not earn.
