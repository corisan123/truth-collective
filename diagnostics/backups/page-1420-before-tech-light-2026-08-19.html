Best Hearing Aids and Sound Amplification Devices

Reviewed across three buyer needs, including professional sound amplification for jobs that demand sharper hearing, discreet daily-wear hearing aids for mild to moderate hearing loss, and clinical-grade OTC options for stronger hearing support without the audiologist price.

Hearing aids are a regulated medical device category. Each hearing aid featured on this page is FDA-registered as an Over-the-Counter (OTC) hearing aid for adults with perceived mild to moderate hearing loss, in accordance with the FDA's 2022 OTC ruling. Sound amplification devices on this page are classified separately by the FDA as Personal Sound Amplification Products (PSAPs) and are intended for healthy hearing in environments where amplification supports clarity, not for the treatment of hearing loss. Truth Collective recommends consulting a licensed audiologist for any clinically diagnosed or moderate-to-severe hearing loss before selecting an OTC device.
