How We Evaluate Wellness Resources

This Is Not a List of What Feels Good. It Is a List of What Has Been Verified.

Mental wellness and self-help resources published in this hub are evaluated against APA clinical and research standards, NIMH evidence-based practice guidelines, and SPJ editorial ethics. Author credentials, evidence-based methodology, citation quality, responsible messaging, and the absence of exploitative claims are all assessed before any resource earns a place here.

Resources go through a 21-point qualitative evaluation covering research depth, factual accuracy, source authority, editorial voice, originality, bias assessment, and overall impact. Content that makes unverified clinical claims, promotes harmful ideation, or lacks credible sourcing is disqualified and will not appear in this hub regardless of popularity or endorsement.

The standard applied here is the same standard used for any clinical reference. People helping people is not a slogan. It is a responsibility that shapes every selection decision in this hub.
