How to Choose the Correct Sound System and Setup:

The single most common mistake in speaker selection is choosing a speaker before defining the acoustic environment it will operate in. Room volume, surface materials, and listening distance determine which speaker type, power rating, and dispersion pattern will perform correctly in your specific space.

A speaker that sounds exceptional in a carpeted 12 x 14 ft home office will sound thin and directionless in a hard surface open plan living area of the same square footage. Before evaluating any speaker on this page, measure your room dimensions, identify your primary listening position relative to the speaker placement, and determine whether your priority is near field accuracy at close range, wide dispersion for multiple listeners, or deep bass extension for music and home theater content.

Those three answers eliminate at least half the categories below before you read a single product description.
