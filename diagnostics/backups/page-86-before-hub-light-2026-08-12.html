The Misconception of Self-Help and Mental Wellness

Self-help is not about pretending everything is fine. It is about finding language for what hurts, a clearer perspective for what feels heavy, and steadier next steps when life feels uncertain. In hard seasons, even a kind word, a calm voice, or the right resource at the right time can matter more than most people realize.
