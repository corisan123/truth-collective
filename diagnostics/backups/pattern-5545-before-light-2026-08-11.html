How Products Earn a Place on The Truth Collective

Every recommendation meets a written standard before it is published. Products must meet a minimum 3.5/5-star rating with a record of long-term reviews. We evaluate build quality, brand track record, durability, and whether the product does its job better than alternatives at its price point. We also check that it is currently available through a trusted retailer with a sensible return path.

Products that do not meet those conditions and more, are not listed. Read our complete Selection Standards to understand how we review, research, grade, and recommend every book, every item, helpful guides, and products on Truth Collective.
