Full-Color Smart Bulbs

The most versatile bulb in any connected system. Full-color smart bulbs deliver millions of color options, adjustable white temperature, and scheduled routines that shape how every room feels through the day. The right color bulb integrates seamlessly with hubs, voice assistants, and circadian programs.

Non-Color Smart Bulbs

The most reliable upgrade for daily living spaces. White-only smart bulbs adjust from warm amber for evening to cool white for daytime focus, supporting natural circadian rhythm without the cost or complexity of full-color options. Built for bedrooms, kitchens, hallways, and home offices.

LED Accent Light Strips

The signature accent of any modern lighting setup. LED strips run behind desks, under cabinets, around mirrors, and along entertainment walls, adding depth and ambient color without harsh overhead glare. Premium strips offer dense LED placement, smooth color transitions, and reliable adhesive that lasts.

Under-Cabinet and Task Strip Lighting

Built for the spaces ordinary bulbs cannot reach. Under-cabinet strips illuminate kitchen counters, workshop benches, and reading nooks with even, glare-free light. The right under-cabinet system installs without wiring, runs on rechargeable batteries or low-voltage adapters, and operates through the same hub as the rest of the home.

Outdoor and Weatherproof Strip Lighting

Strip lighting built for exterior use. Weatherproof outdoor strips line decks, pergolas, fences, and pathways with durable, IP-rated construction that survives rain, snow, and direct sun. Built for permanent installation and integrated control with the rest of the smart lighting system.

Smart Bulb and Strip Starter Systems

Complete starter kits that simplify the first smart lighting purchase. Premium starter systems pair bulbs, strips, and the required hub in one package, removing the guesswork of compatibility and giving new users a working system from the first day.
