Our Selection Standard

Every Recommendation Earns Its Place.
None of Them Are Given It.

Truth Collective evaluates every product, book, software platform, and resource against internationally recognized engineering and editorial standards, including IEEE, BIFMA, IES, APA, and FTC guidelines. The process is a multi-stage technical audit, not a rating. Products are scored, tiered, and either recommended or disqualified based on evidence alone.

No affiliate relationship, sponsorship, or paid placement changes the outcome. Only products and titles scoring 3.5 or above on the TC grading scale are eligible for publication. The full framework, governing bodies, scoring weights, tier definitions, and hub-specific criteria, is documented in the TC Unified Governance Binder.
