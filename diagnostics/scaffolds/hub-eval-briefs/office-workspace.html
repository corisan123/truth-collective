How We Evaluate Office Workspace Products

Your Body Will Work at This Desk for Decades. We Evaluated It Accordingly.

Office workspace products are evaluated against BIFMA X5.1 load testing standards, NHLA material grading rules for wood products, AWI joinery specifications, and ANSI ergonomic performance criteria. ISO 9001 manufacturing quality standards apply across all categories. Products that cannot document BIFMA load compliance or that use sub-grade materials are disqualified at Stage I.

Stage II scores material grade and density, structural joinery quality, load distribution engineering, hardware grade, surface durability, and ergonomic certification. Scoring weights component engineering at 30%. In furniture, that means joinery method, fastener quality, and internal bracing, not finish color or marketing photography.

A product that passes a lifestyle shoot is not the same product as one that passes BIFMA. The products in this hub have passed the standard that matters.
