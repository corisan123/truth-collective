About Iron Sharpens Iron Consulting

Clarity first. Action second. Results always.

I have spent more than three decades building business, leading teams, and solving problems across manufacturing, construction, automation, and business development. I have opened markets from zero. I have built pipelines from nothing. I have rebuilt underperforming operations and helped companies regain momentum when it mattered most.

My work is direct, diagnostic, and grounded in real-world execution. I do not bring pre-packaged frameworks or corporate theater. I bring clarity, structure, and the discipline to get to the truth: the truth leaders need in order to move forward.

I have led national division launches, created multimillion-dollar revenue streams, delivered 48M in automation and robotics transactions, and collaborated with teams achieving year-over-year EBITDA improvements between 21% and 67%. I have trained multi-region teams, written national Sales/Ops SOPs, and built systems that continue performing long after implementation.

I work with founders, private equity firms, C-suite executives, and Fortune 500 organizations. My clients are leaders who want to get better, not leaders who want to be told they are already great.

Iron Sharpens Iron is not a slogan. It is how I work.

Based in North Carolina. Remote. Nationwide.
