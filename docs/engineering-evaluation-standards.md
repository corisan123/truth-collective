# Truth Collective — Engineering Evaluation Standards

Source: TC Master Engineering Driven Evaluation Systems (Draft 2.1.1, July 2026),
by Daniel Reid, MBA, LEED, PMP. Classification: Public.

This is the grading methodology behind the Truth Collective Grade. Grades are
evidence-based. No manipulated ratings. No paid endorsements. This is why the
brand can say it does not make anything up.

## Summary

Truth Collective evaluates engineered products using a dual-stage methodology
grounded in internationally recognized manufacturing standards, engineering
benchmarks, material specifications, and performance metrics.

## 1. Governing Bodies and Standards Organizations

- Global manufacturing and quality: ISO 9001, ISO/IEC standards, IATF 16949, AS9100
- Safety and compliance: UL, ETL, CSA Group, FCC, CPSC, DOE, EPA
- Category-specific engineering: AES, IEEE, JEDEC, PCI-SIG, AWI, NHLA, ANSI / ASC O1

## 2. Technical Benchmark Framework

Multi-layered evaluation across material quality, construction integrity,
component engineering, electrical performance, category-specific performance,
safety, and compliance.

## 3. Selection Framework

Candidates are selected on category relevance, availability, stated
specifications, compliance claims, market visibility, and alignment with
professional-use standards. Selection emphasizes objective, measurable evidence
rather than marketing claims, consumer star ratings, or price positioning.

Benchmark areas: Material Quality, Structural Integrity and Construction,
Component Engineering, Electrical Performance, Category-Specific Performance.

## 4. Testing Methodology

Test phases:

1. Intake and eligibility screening
2. Standards mapping against relevant governing bodies
3. Technical benchmark review
4. Category-specific performance evaluation
5. In-house expert review
6. Tier assignment and recommendation review
7. Transparency, disclosure, and publication readiness check

Acceptance metrics: Standards Alignment, Engineering Quality, Performance
Consistency, Safety and Compliance, Professional-Use Fit.

## 5. Truth Collective Tiering System

- Tier 1 — Premium Engineering Grade: Meets or exceeds all major technical benchmarks, top-tier materials, superior construction, full compliance certification.
- Tier 2 — Professional Grade: Meets most benchmarks, high-quality components, reliable construction with minor limitations.
- Tier 3 — Consumer Grade: Meets minimum standards, mid-tier materials, acceptable construction for general use.
- Tier 4 — Entry Level: Fails multiple benchmarks, low-grade materials, or lacks compliance certification.

## 6. Category-Specific Benchmark Addendum

Covered categories include Woodworking and Joinery, Audio Engineering, Computing
Hardware, Smart Home and AI Wearables, and Lighting, Ergonomics and Productivity.

## 7. Transparency and Consumer Protection

- Adherence to FTC endorsement guidelines
- Full disclosure of affiliate relationships
- Independent testing and evaluation standards
- No paid endorsements
- No manipulated ratings

## 8. Benchmark Sheet

A full benchmark sheet is provided per category containing product
identification, standards mapping, benchmark results, in-house evaluation, and
tier rationale.

## Implementation note for schema and display

- The Grade is displayed through the `tc-grade-block` HTML class, not written text (see Master Brief section 8).
- Any rating or review structured data must reflect the real Truth Collective Grade or the documented retailer rating. Never generate rating or review schema from invented numbers.
