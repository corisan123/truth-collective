## Selection criteria (from binder §1.2 — product Quantitative track)

Minimum thresholds used in this monitor binder (category-specific; pattern generalizes):
- Resolution: 4K UHD minimum; 5K/6K preferred for Mac
- Panel: IPS, IPS Black, or OLED only (gaming monitors excluded for executive lists)
- Color accuracy: Delta E &lt; 2 (factory verified or independently tested)
- Connectivity: USB-C with minimum 90W PD strongly preferred
- Ergonomics: height, tilt, swivel; portrait pivot preferred
- User rating: 4.1+ aggregated, interpreted via scoring framework (not raw stars as truth)

**#1 executive pick in binder:** Dell UltraSharp U2725QE (IPS Black, 27", 4K, 120Hz, TB4 140W)

## Scoring doctrine (non-subjective framing)

- Stars are **ordinal sentiment**, not quality percent
- **Expectation gap** at premium price tiers systematically deflates good products
- Prefer histogram / 80/10 rule / review language over face-value aggregates
- Cross-check independent labs (RTINGS, TFT Central, etc.)
