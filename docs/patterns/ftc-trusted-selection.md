## Copy (verbatim)

Trusted Selection Standards heading:

> Truth Collective's Trusted Selection Standards

Sub heading:

> Stage II of TC Selection Standards

Four-point selection paragraph:

> Every product featured across Truth Collective is reviewed against Truth Collective's trusted selection process before it earns a place on the page. Products must meet a minimum 3.5/5 star rating with a record of long-term reviews, demonstrate build quality and brand track record worthy of daily professional use, do one job better than alternatives at their price point, and remain available through a trusted retailer with a sensible return path and elevated customer service.

Grade independence paragraph:

> Truth Collective grades reflect our independent review process. While some products may display different ratings on retailer pages, Truth Collective grades are assigned through our own testing, research, and editorial review, weighed against the four-point Trusted Selection Process. We firmly stand behind our rankings as honest, independent assessments built for the long-term trust of our readers.

FTC paragraph (contains the external link):

> Truth Collective also follows the Federal Trade Commission's endorsement and testimonial guidelines. Every recommendation is honest, every affiliate relationship is disclosed, and no product receives a placement it did not earn. You can review the [FTC Endorsement Guides for Consumer Protection](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) here.

## Notes

- The grade-independence paragraph already establishes that Truth Collective grades every recommendation, which supports grading books, not only engineered products.
- Editorial byline used with this pattern: "By: Truth Collective Editorial | Updated [Month Year] | Reviewed against our Trusted Selection Standards".
- Attribution line seen with the pattern: "Every recommendation is independently selected, reviewed, and aligned with our Trusted Selection Standards."
